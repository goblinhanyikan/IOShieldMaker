Plate (mm)

Body = part that fits the case opening. Rim = flange around it, outside the case. Case metal = sheet thickness. Catches = snap ledges on all four sides (light purple in the preview).

Ports

Preset sizes are approximate. Match them to your photo.

Align

Snapping uses port centers and edges. Ports stay axis-aligned, so a slanted line moves their centers onto it. Drag the line handles to adjust it.

Export

Prints flange-down with the outside face on the bed, no flipping. The flange stays outside the case, the body goes through the opening, and the ledges catch behind the case metal. Clearance is added to every cutout.
